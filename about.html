ABOUT SENTINEL

Calm, disciplined, and practical technology support.

Sentinel Infrastructure Solutions was created to help small organizations operate with more reliable, secure, and understandable technology.

THE COMPANY

Built from experience in high-reliability environments.

Sentinel Infrastructure Solutions is a veteran-owned IT consulting business based in Winter Garden, Florida. The company is led by Jason Tourangeau, an Army combat veteran and information technology student with experience across military operations, emergency services, federal facility security, healthcare, municipal infrastructure, technical troubleshooting, and customer support.

Those environments share a common requirement: systems, people, and procedures must work when they are needed. That operational mindset guides every Sentinel recommendation.
