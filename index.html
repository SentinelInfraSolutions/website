COMMON QUESTIONS

Start with a clear conversation.

Do you work only with businesses in Winter Garden?

No. Local service is available in Central Florida, and many Microsoft 365, planning, security, and troubleshooting services can be provided remotely.

Do you support home offices and very small businesses?

Yes. Sentinel is designed to help small organizations and home-based businesses build more professional, reliable, and secure technology environments.

Can you help even if I do not know exactly what I need?

Yes. The first step is understanding the problem and your goals. You will receive plain-language options before any work is agreed upon.

Do you sell equipment?

Equipment can be recommended and planned as part of a project. Recommendations are based on fit, value, security, and maintainability rather than a single brand.
